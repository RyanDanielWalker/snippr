Snippr 1.5 is about using it without a mouse, or without seeing the screen. Every snippet can now be moved to a folder from the keyboard, cards make sense to a screen reader, and on a phone, toasts stop covering the buttons you need. Try it at [snippr.dev](https://snippr.dev). The demo needs no account.

## What you'll notice

- **Move, on every card.** It opens a short list: Unorganized, then each folder. Pick one and the snippet moves there. It works with a mouse, from the keyboard, and on a phone, where a finger drag scrolls the page instead. Before, the only way without dragging was to open the snippet, press Edit, pick a folder, and save. Dragging a card onto a folder still works. ([0073](https://github.com/RyanDanielWalker/snippr/blob/v1.5/docs/adr/0073-move-a-snippet-from-a-move-menu-not-a-keyboard-drag.md))
- **On a phone, a toast no longer covers a dialog's buttons.** Right after a delete, the "Moved to Recently deleted." toast stays 8 seconds. It used to sit on top of the next preview's Edit and Delete for that whole time. Dialogs on a phone now leave room at the bottom, and the toast lands there. ([0074](https://github.com/RyanDanielWalker/snippr/blob/v1.5/docs/adr/0074-leave-room-for-toasts-at-the-bottom-of-phone-dialogs.md))

## For screen reader and keyboard users

- **A snippet card is an article, and its title is the button that opens it.** The whole card used to be one button with Edit, Copy, and Delete inside it, so a screen reader read the card's entire text as one button's name and might not reach the buttons inside at all. Now each button is its own, and a screen reader can jump from card to card. A click anywhere on a card still opens it. ([0072](https://github.com/RyanDanielWalker/snippr/blob/v1.5/docs/adr/0072-snippet-cards-are-articles-opened-by-a-title-button.md))
- **A drag announces names, not ids.** During a mouse drag, a screen reader used to hear "Picked up draggable item" followed by the snippet's database id. Now it hears "Picked up Debounce.", "Over Shell.", and "Dropped Debounce on Shell." ([0073](https://github.com/RyanDanielWalker/snippr/blob/v1.5/docs/adr/0073-move-a-snippet-from-a-move-menu-not-a-keyboard-drag.md))
- **The phone folder drawer is a proper dialog.** Focus moves into it when it opens and back to the Folders button when it closes, and the page behind it can't be reached, so swiping past the last folder no longer lands on the cards underneath. Escape while renaming a folder now cancels the rename without closing the drawer. ([0071](https://github.com/RyanDanielWalker/snippr/blob/v1.5/docs/adr/0071-close-1-4-and-scope-1-5-to-accessibility.md))
