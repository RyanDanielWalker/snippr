Snippr 1.3 finishes the archive that 1.2 started, and makes delete something you can take back. Try it at [snippr.dev](https://snippr.dev). The demo needs no account.

## What you'll notice

- **Recently deleted.** Deleting a snippet no longer removes it straight away. It moves to a Recently deleted row in the sidebar, under Archive, and stays there for 30 days. Each card there says how long it has left ("Gone in 12 days") and offers Restore, Copy, and Delete forever. Restore puts a snippet back in its folder, or back in the archive if it was archived. If its folder was deleted in the meantime, it goes to Unorganized. A deleted snippet doesn't count toward the 50-snippet limit, so deleting still makes room straight away. That's also why restoring is refused while the library is full. Recently deleted holds as many snippets as the library does, and one more delete past that removes the oldest for good. AI search leaves deleted snippets out, in the app and over MCP. ([0067](https://github.com/RyanDanielWalker/snippr/blob/v1.3/docs/adr/0067-recently-deleted-a-30-day-trash-emptied-when-the-library-loads.md))
- **Drop a card on Archive to archive it.** Archive was a button only. The Archive row is now a drop target, in the full sidebar and on the collapsed rail, and it shows during a drag even while nothing is archived yet. On a phone, where cards can't be dragged, the button is still the way. ([0065](https://github.com/RyanDanielWalker/snippr/blob/v1.3/docs/adr/0065-close-1-2-1-and-scope-1-3-to-finishing-the-archive.md))

## Fixed

- **A folder's delete confirmation counts its archived snippets.** It said how many snippets would move to Unorganized and left out the archived ones, which move too. Now it names both: "Its 3 snippets move to Unorganized. Its 2 archived snippets stay archived, without a folder." A folder that held only archived snippets used to show no note at all. ([0056](https://github.com/RyanDanielWalker/snippr/blob/v1.3/docs/adr/0056-archive-snippets-with-an-archivedat-column.md))
- **Screen readers can tell which sidebar row is open.** The open row was marked by its color only. It now also says "current" to a screen reader, in the full sidebar, the phone drawer, and the rail. That matters most on All snippets, which has no heading above the cards to say where you are.

## Underneath

- **Nothing runs on a schedule to empty the trash.** When you open your dashboard, your own snippets that have been in Recently deleted for over 30 days are removed first. The query that does it only ever touches the snippets of the person loading the page, so a mistake in it can't reach anyone else's. Tests against a real Postgres check exactly that. ([0067](https://github.com/RyanDanielWalker/snippr/blob/v1.3/docs/adr/0067-recently-deleted-a-30-day-trash-emptied-when-the-library-loads.md))
- **The code was audited before the tag.** It was the first code audit since before 1.0. Nothing was high severity. It found five small things, and all five were fixed before this release: three places where the docs or code comments had fallen behind the code, one React pattern used in nine places that was never explained, and the screen reader fix above. ([0068](https://github.com/RyanDanielWalker/snippr/blob/v1.3/docs/adr/0068-audit-the-code-before-tagging-1-3.md), [audit](https://github.com/RyanDanielWalker/snippr/blob/v1.3/docs/audits/2026-11-18.md))
