Snippr 1.4 finishes Recently deleted, the trash 1.3 added. Delete takes one press and can be undone from the toast, and the trash can be emptied in one go. Try it at [snippr.dev](https://snippr.dev). The demo needs no account.

## What you'll notice

- **Delete takes one press, and the toast offers Undo.** Delete used to ask twice. A deleted snippet waits 30 days in Recently deleted, so the second press guarded against a mistake that's easy to fix. Now Delete acts at once on the card, in the preview, and in the edit form. The toast says "Moved to Recently deleted." with an Undo button, and Undo puts the snippet back where it was. A toast with a button stays 8 seconds, and waits while your pointer is over it. Restore in Recently deleted still works for the full 30 days. Delete forever still asks twice, since it can't be undone. ([0070](https://github.com/RyanDanielWalker/snippr/blob/v1.4/docs/adr/0070-one-press-delete-with-undo-and-an-empty-button.md))
- **Empty clears Recently deleted.** It sits beside the Recently deleted heading and asks twice before it deletes everything there for good. Before, each snippet had to be deleted forever one at a time, or left for its 30 days. ([0070](https://github.com/RyanDanielWalker/snippr/blob/v1.4/docs/adr/0070-one-press-delete-with-undo-and-an-empty-button.md))

## Underneath

- **Empty has its own address, `DELETE /api/trash`.** It takes no body, so there's nothing to validate and no way to mix it up with deleting one snippet. Its query only matches the signed-in user's deleted snippets. Tests against a real Postgres check that with two users. ([0070](https://github.com/RyanDanielWalker/snippr/blob/v1.4/docs/adr/0070-one-press-delete-with-undo-and-an-empty-button.md))
